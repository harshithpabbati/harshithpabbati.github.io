Nidar, a PWA .The app uses a safeness heatmap and zoning to recommend the safest route, through data mining done on historical crime data, proximity to police stations, bars etc, and the incidents reported through the app. The app also allows to report incidences at a tap of button right from the notification bar, hassle-free and takes it up with the authorities.
